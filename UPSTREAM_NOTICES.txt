BINANCE VISION
Market prices and execution-derived statistics are based on Binance Vision
historical public spot trades: https://data.binance.vision/ .
Documentation: https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data .
Terms: https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/blob/master/TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md .

The current terms specify CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for the datasets and derived analytical
indicators, including attribution and ShareAlike requirements for redistributed
derivatives. Binance-derived data and results in this package carry that license:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/ .
Changes: executions were windowed around recorded event times; prices, quantities,
and purchase windows were transformed into clock-specific returns, bounds, and
descriptive/inferential statistics. The raw Binance archives are not bundled.
No Binance sponsorship or endorsement is asserted.
